01 — Prisma Grid

Squares that
track your cursor

Dense field of small squares rotating exactly toward the mouse using Math.atan2 — gold glow on approach, 2-second fade out on leave.

02 — Glow Cards

Light that follows
your hand

Each card paints a radial beam precisely where you hover — illuminating from within, clipped to its own boundary.

03 — Ripple Grid

Touch that
spreads outward

Every cell triggers a euclidean ripple wave — scale and color cascade to neighbouring cells in a timed sequence.

04 — Magnetic Buttons

Buttons that
pull toward you

Cursor proximity warps each button's position — with a full fill animation that triggers on close approach.

05 — Text Scramble

Characters that
reveal themselves

Hover any line to trigger a random character scramble that resolves back to the original text — chaotic then precise.

06 — Infinite Ticker

Endless motion
in both directions

Dual-direction infinite marquee — pauses on hover. Seamless looping without JavaScript, pure CSS animation.

07 — 3D Tilt Cards

Depth through
perspective

CSS perspective transforms driven by mouse position — a specular highlight layer adds the illusion of physical surface.
